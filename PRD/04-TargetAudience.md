# Purpose

This document identifies the users of CampusEsports, their characteristics, needs, challenges, motivations, and how the platform will provide value to each audience segment.

---

# Audience Overview

CampusEsports is designed for students, organizers, colleges, gaming clubs, and sponsors involved in college esports.

The platform initially focuses on engineering colleges before expanding to universities and institutions across India.

---

# Primary Target Audience

## College Students

### Age

17–25 Years

### Education

- Engineering Colleges
- Universities
- Degree Colleges

### Interests

- BGMI
- Free Fire
- Valorant
- CS2
- FIFA
- Rocket League
- Call of Duty Mobile

### Goals

- Participate in tournaments
- Build teams
- Improve gaming skills
- Win prizes
- Gain recognition

### Pain Points

- Difficult registrations
- Poor communication
- No centralized platform
- Manual tournament management

---

# Secondary Target Audience

## Tournament Organizers

### Responsibilities

- Create tournaments
- Verify registrations
- Manage teams
- Schedule matches
- Publish results

### Challenges

- Manual registrations
- Spreadsheet management
- Communication issues
- Time-consuming bracket creation

CampusEsports automates these processes.

---

# College Administrators

These users oversee official college events.

### Goals

- Organize professional tournaments
- Improve student engagement
- Track participation
- Promote campus esports

### Benefits

- Analytics dashboard
- Event reports
- Attendance tracking
- Tournament history

---

# Gaming Clubs

Campus gaming clubs organize regular competitions and practice sessions.

Needs:

- Team management
- Club announcements
- Member directory
- Internal tournaments

---

# Sponsors

Brands interested in college gaming communities.

Examples:

- Gaming peripherals
- Energy drinks
- Internet providers
- Gaming cafes
- PC brands

Benefits

- Brand visibility
- Tournament sponsorship
- Student engagement
- Marketing campaigns

# Audience Needs Summary

Students need:

- Easy registration
- Team creation
- Live updates
- Player profiles
- Rankings

Organizers need:

- Tournament automation
- Team management
- Match scheduling
- Analytics

Colleges need:

- Event management
- Reports
- Student engagement

Sponsors need:

- Marketing opportunities
- Brand visibility
- Audience insights

---

# Conclusion

CampusEsports serves multiple stakeholders within the college esports ecosystem.

By addressing the needs of students, organizers, colleges, and sponsors through one integrated platform, CampusEsports creates a scalable and sustainable ecosystem capable of expanding across India.
