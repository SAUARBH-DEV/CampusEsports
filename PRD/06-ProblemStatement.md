# Purpose

This document identifies the major problems faced by students, tournament organizers, colleges, and gaming communities. It explains why these problems exist and how Kurukshetra aims to solve them.

---

# Current Situation

College esports tournaments in India are growing rapidly, but the organization process remains largely manual.

Most tournaments rely on:

- WhatsApp
- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- Discord
- Manual bracket creation

These methods become difficult to manage as participation increases.

---

# Problems Faced by Students

Students experience several challenges:

- No centralized tournament platform
- Confusing registration process
- Match schedule updates are inconsistent
- Difficulty finding teammates
- No player profiles or rankings
- No tournament history
- Limited recognition for achievements

---

# Problems Faced by Tournament Organizers

Organizers spend significant time on manual tasks:

- Managing registrations
- Verifying participants
- Creating brackets
- Scheduling matches
- Announcing results
- Handling disputes
- Sending reminders

This increases workload and the chance of errors.

---

# Problems Faced by Colleges

Colleges often lack:

- Centralized esports management
- Tournament records
- Participation analytics
- Student performance tracking
- Standardized tournament processes

---

# Problems Faced by Gaming Communities

Gaming communities face challenges such as:

- Fragmented communication
- No long-term player profiles
- Limited discovery of talented players
- Difficulty organizing inter-college events

---

# Root Causes

The core reasons behind these problems include:

- Lack of a dedicated college esports platform
- Heavy dependence on manual workflows
- Use of multiple disconnected tools
- Absence of standardized tournament management

---

# Impact

These issues lead to:

- Reduced participation
- Organizer frustration
- Poor player experience
- Missed opportunities for talent discovery
- Inefficient event management

---

# Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current platforms either focus on:

- Communication only
- Brackets only
- Registration only

No single platform combines registration, tournament management, communities, player profiles, analytics, and college-focused workflows into one solution.

---

# How Kurukshetra Solves These Problems

Kurukshetra provides:

- Digital registrations
- Team management
- Automated brackets
- Live notifications
- Player profiles
- College communities
- Organizer dashboard
- Analytics
- Tournament history
- Rankings

This creates one unified platform for the college esports ecosystem.

---

# Success Criteria

Kurukshetra will be successful when:

- Tournament setup time decreases significantly.
- Students can register in minutes.
- Organizers spend less time on manual work.
- Colleges can track tournament participation.
- Players build long-term gaming profiles.

---

# Conclusion

The current ecosystem lacks an integrated platform designed specifically for Indian college esports.

By addressing the challenges faced by students, organizers, colleges, and gaming communities, Kurukshetra aims to become the standard platform for organizing and managing college esports tournaments across India.
