# Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the Kurukshetra platform, its objectives, core features, and how it addresses the challenges faced by students, organizers, colleges, and sponsors in the college esports ecosystem.

---

# Product Overview

Kurukshetra is a modern college esports platform designed to simplify tournament organization and participation.

The platform enables students, organizers, colleges, and gaming communities to manage every aspect of esports competitions from one centralized system.

Instead of relying on WhatsApp, Google Forms, and spreadsheets, Kurukshetra provides an integrated experience with registrations, team management, live brackets, notifications, analytics, and player profiles.

---

# Vision of the Solution

Kurukshetra aims to become the digital infrastructure for college esports in India by providing a secure, scalable, and user-friendly platform that connects players, organizers, institutions, and sponsors.

---

The first version of Kurukshetra will include:

The platform supports multiple user roles:

- Create teams
- Track rankings

### Organizer

### College Administrator
