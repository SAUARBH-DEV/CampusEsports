# Design Implications

The personas suggest that CampusEsports should provide:

- Mobile-first design
- Fast registration
- Dark gaming theme
- Live notifications
- Easy navigation
- Modern dashboard
- Simple tournament creation
- Accessible interface

---

# Conclusion

The identified personas represent the core users of CampusEsports.

Understanding their goals, frustrations, and behaviors helps ensure the platform delivers a user experience that meets the needs of students, organizers, colleges, and sponsors while supporting future growth.
