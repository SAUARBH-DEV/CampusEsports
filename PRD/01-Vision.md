# Vision Statement


To become India's leading college esports platform that connects students, colleges, and gaming communities through organized, competitive, and accessible tournaments while creating opportunities for players, organizers, and esports enthusiasts.

---

# Mission Statement

# Mission Statement

CampusEsports aims to simplify the organization of college esports tournaments by providing a modern digital platform where students can register, create teams, participate in tournaments, track match schedules, view live brackets, and build their gaming profiles in a fair and engaging environment.

---

# Problem We Are Solving

# Problem We Are Solving

Current college esports tournaments face several challenges:

- Registration is managed through WhatsApp or Google Forms.
- Team management is manual and time-consuming.
- Match schedules are difficult to organize.
- Tournament brackets are often created manually.
- Players receive poor communication about match timings.
- There is no centralized platform dedicated to college esports.
- Colleges lack tools to manage tournaments efficiently.

---

# Our Solution

# Our Solution

CampusEsports is a dedicated tournament management platform that enables students and organizers to:

- Register online
- Create and manage teams
- Join tournaments
- View tournament brackets
- Receive match notifications
- Track leaderboards
- Build gaming profiles
- Enable organizers to manage tournaments efficiently
