# Purpose

This document analyzes the Indian esports market, identifies opportunities in college esports, evaluates competitors, estimates market size, and explains why CampusEsports has the potential to become the leading college esports platform.


# Industry Overview

Esports has evolved from a niche hobby into a multi-billion-dollar global industry. Competitive gaming is now recognized as a professional sport in many countries, supported by tournament organizers, sponsors, streaming platforms, and gaming publishers.

India is one of the fastest-growing gaming markets due to affordable smartphones, low-cost internet, and a young population. However, organized college esports remains largely underserved, with most tournaments still managed manually.

# Indian Esports Market

India has one of the largest gaming populations in the world.

Growth drivers include:

- Affordable smartphones
- Cheap mobile internet
- Popular multiplayer games
- Growth of gaming creators
- Increased investment from brands
- Rising interest in competitive gaming

The Indian esports ecosystem continues to expand through national tournaments, university competitions, and professional organizations.
